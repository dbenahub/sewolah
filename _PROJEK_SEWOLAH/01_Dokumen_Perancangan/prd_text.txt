1. OBJECTIVE
Build a high-converting premium landing page for SEWOLAH.
Primary objective: Convert visitors into qualified booking enquiries through a booking form.
After the form is submitted:
Save the lead information.
Trigger the Lead conversion event.
Redirect customer to WhatsApp.
Pre-fill WhatsApp with the information submitted in the form.
Main conversion flow:
Meta Ads / Social Media → Landing Page → Booking Form → Submit → WhatsApp → SEWOLAH Team Follow-Up → Quotation → Booking Confirmation
